# Trophy models

Drop a GLB named after the trophy's `key` in `src/dirs/tunnel/trophies.js` (e.g. `kings.glb`) and set
`model: '/models/trophies/<key>.glb'` on that entry. The trophy room scales it to its case, stands it
on its base, gives it the room's reflections and turns it; until the file exists the stylised form shows.
